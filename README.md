# 💇 Hair Inspo Pro

Discover your perfect hairstyle with the power of AI.

**Hair Inspo Pro** is an AI-powered virtual hairstyling app that helps you explore different hairstyles and apply realistic wigs before making a real change. Whether you're looking for a fresh haircut, a new hair color, or the perfect wig, Hair Inspo Pro lets you preview your look in seconds.

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 AI-powered hairstyle generation
- 💇 Try different hairstyles instantly
- 👩 Apply realistic virtual wigs
- 🎨 Explore multiple hair colors and styles
- 📸 Upload your photo for personalized results
- ⚡ Fast and easy-to-use interface
- 💎 Premium hairstyles and features with subscription

## 🚀 Why Hair Inspo Pro?

Choosing a new hairstyle can be difficult. Hair Inspo Pro makes it easy by allowing you to visualize different looks before committing. Experiment with confidence, discover your next style, and find the perfect look that suits you.

## 📱 Requirements

- iOS 15.0+
- Internet connection required for AI processing

## 💳 Subscription

Hair Inspo Pro offers optional premium subscriptions that unlock:

- Unlimited AI hairstyle generations
- Premium hairstyle collection
- Exclusive wig styles
- Faster AI processing
- Ad-free experience

Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled through your App Store account.
